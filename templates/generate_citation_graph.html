Builds a directed graph of the articles in the current database. Edges are oriented citing → cited. Nodes are coloured by the iteration in which they were first discovered. Outputs are a GraphML and GEXF file (for Gephi / Cytoscape), a nodes/edges JSON, and an interactive HTML view.
Union of recorded snowball edges and edges reconstructed from the API.
Reconstruction queries Semantic Scholar once per paper and can take several minutes on a large database. Results are cached, so re-running is fast.
